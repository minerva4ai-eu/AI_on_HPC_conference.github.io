The MINERVA Supercomputing Conference brings together researchers, engineers, and infrastructure providers to explore how High Performance Computing (HPC) is enabling the next generation of AI.

About the conference

The rapid growth of large-scale AI, from foundation model pretraining to multimodal architecture, increasingly relies on the computational power, scalability, and expertise found in High Performance Computing (HPC) centers. This conference brings together researchers, engineers, and infrastructure providers to explore the evolving relationship between AI and HPC, showcasing how supercomputing resources are enabling the next generation of AI systems.

Organized by MINERVA, a project designed to bring together HPC and AI communities, the conference highlights the importance of collaboration between supercomputing centers, research institutions, and industry in advancing open and scalable AI innovation across Europe.

When and where

The conference will take place at the Bologna Tecnopolo on 23–24 February 2027.

Participation

Participation will be completely free of charge. Registration will open soon on the Registration page

Who should attend

The event is intended for HPC engineers, AI researchers, research software engineers, infrastructure teams, industry partners, and anyone working at the intersection of scalable computing and AI.

Main themes

  • Distributed training and AI at scale on supercomputing systems
  • Infrastructure and software stacks for AI workloads in HPC environments
  • Open foundation models and collaborative model development
  • European collaboration across supercomputing centers, research institutions, and industry
  • Posters, workshops, and technical exchange between HPC and AI communities
