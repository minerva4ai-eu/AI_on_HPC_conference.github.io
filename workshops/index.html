Workshops

The conference includes four technical workshops led by MINERVA partners.

Generative Multimodal Models on HPC: From Text-to-Image Diffusion to Diffusion Language Models

Partner: UNIMORE

A practical, 2-hour technical session on the generative models behind today’s multimodal AI, and on how to train and run them on HPC infrastructure. The first part covers text-to-image diffusion transformers: how they are built, what goes on inside them, and how they can be steered and adapted. The second part covers multimodal LLMs, from the autoregressive LLaVA-style recipe to recent diffusion language models (LLaDA, Dream, SDAR) that generate text by iterative denoising instead of left-to-right decoding. In the hands-on part, participants will run multi-GPU training and inference jobs on Leonardo through SLURM. They will study the trade-offs between denoising steps, parallel decoding, output quality and latency, and compare the results against autoregressive baselines on standard benchmarks.

Efficient LLM Training on HPC: Scalability, Performance & Optimization

Partner: Barcelona Supercomputing Centre (BSC)

A practical, 2-hour technical deep dive into scaling Large Language Model (LLM) training on HPC infrastructure. Participants will learn how to deploy training jobs on big scale for medium to big size models, identify hardware and distributed bottlenecks using modern profiling tools, and apply memory and execution optimizations across multi-GPU and multi-node setups.
