Preliminary program

The detailed timetable, speakers, and workshop titles will be announced soon. The outline below presents the thematic structure currently planned for the two conference days.

Day 1 · Tuesday, 23 February 2027

09:00 Registration and welcome coffee
10:00 Opening remarks and conference welcome
10:30 Introduction to the goals of the event and the role of MINERVA in connecting AI and HPC communities across Europe.
11:00 Technical talks: AI training at scale
11:30 Sessions focused on large-scale training workflows, scalability challenges, and the use of supercomputing systems for modern AI pipelines.
13:00 Lunch break
14:30 Partner workshops
15:00 Hands-on and technical workshop sessions led by partner organizations on infrastructure, tools, and operational practices for AI on HPC platforms.
16:30 Poster session and networking
17:30 An interactive session for discussion, project exchange, and community building across research, engineering, and industry participants.

Day 2 · Wednesday, 24 February 2027

09:30 Coffee and day-two welcome
10:00 Infrastructure and software stacks for AI workloads
10:30 Talks and case studies on runtimes, orchestration, storage, software environments, and operational support for demanding AI workloads on HPC systems.
11:30 Open foundation models and collaborative development
12:00 Discussion of open and scalable AI innovation, model development workflows, and shared European efforts in foundation model research.
13:00 Lunch break
14:30 Bridging HPC infrastructure and AI applications
15:00 Sessions dedicated to collaborative frameworks that connect infrastructure capabilities with applied AI development and deployment.
16:00 Closing panel and final discussion
17:00 A concluding conversation on future opportunities for AI-enabled supercomputing and continued collaboration between communities.
