Valeria Zuccoli

Intella

Statistician by education, Valeria is an AI Scientist specializing in real-time models for complex, challenging scenarios. Driven by a deep curiosity for the latest research, she applies advanced analytical techniques to deliver effective solutions in high-impact domains, such as video-surveillance and satellite operations.

Thomas van Osch

SURF

As a member of the high-performance machine learning team, Thomas focuses on streamlining model architectures from a performance and user-friendly perspective. He has worked on efficient data loading projects for EuroCC and is part of the young generation’s involvement in EuroCC collaborations. On the side, Thomas finds interests in computational photography.

Alessandro Ferrari

Argo Vision

Alessandro Ferrari is an AI entrepreneur, researcher, and lecturer with over 15 years of experience in Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence. In 2016, he founded ARGO Vision, an AI startup focused on industrial Computer Vision, which was acquired by the Marchesini Group in 2020. He currently serves as CEO of ARGO Vision and, since 2022, as Adjunct Professor at the University of Pavia, where he teaches Artificial Intelligence and Communication. He is also the founder and organizer of Py4AI, an international AI conference that brings leading researchers, scientists, and practitioners from around the world to Pavia. LinkedIn.
