Venue

The conference will take place at the Bologna Tecnopolo, one of Europe’s leading locations for supercomputing, data-intensive research, and AI infrastructure activities.

Address

Bologna Tecnopolo
Via Stalingrado 84/3
40128 Bologna, Italy

Conference dates

23–24 February 2027

Registration

Participation is free of charge. Registration will open soon on the MINERVA website.

Getting there

  • From Bologna Centrale railway station: local public transport and taxis provide direct access to the Tecnopolo area.
  • From Bologna Guglielmo Marconi Airport: the venue can be reached by taxi or by combining airport and city public transport connections.
  • By car: Bologna Tecnopolo is located in the city and can be reached easily from the main road network around Bologna.
  • Accommodation: attendees are encouraged to book accommodation in Bologna city, with convenient access to the venue by public transport or taxi.

Practical information

Additional details about venue access, on-site registration, and logistical information will be published closer to the event. For updates, follow the MINERVA LinkedIn page.
